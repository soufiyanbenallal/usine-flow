Build a modern, premium SaaS landing page for **Industrial OS**, a Morocco-focused factory, workshop, warehouse, and industrial operations management platform.

The product helps factories and industrial businesses manage **inventory, warehouses, purchasing, sales, production, BOMs, MRP, quality, maintenance, workforce, traceability, costing, and operations** from one system.

The landing page must feel like a **serious modern B2B SaaS product**, not a generic ERP website and not a template copied from a startup landing page.

## Product positioning

Primary positioning:

**Industrial operations, finally under control.**

Supporting message:

**Manage stock, production, purchasing, quality, maintenance, and warehouse operations from one intelligent platform built for modern factories and workshops.**

The landing page should immediately communicate:

- Built for factories and workshops
- Built for warehouses and industrial businesses
- Centralized operations
- Real-time visibility
- Production + inventory + quality + maintenance
- Multi-site and multi-warehouse
- Designed for growing Moroccan businesses
- Simple enough for operators, powerful enough for management

Avoid excessive use of the word “ERP”. Present the product as an **Industrial Operating System**.

# Visual direction

Create a highly polished interface inspired by the visual quality of:

- Linear
- Vercel
- Stripe
- Notion
- Ramp
- Attio
- modern industrial software
- premium enterprise SaaS dashboards

But do NOT clone any of them.

The visual language should combine:

**Modern SaaS + industrial precision + calm enterprise confidence.**

### Design principles

- Light-first design
- Very clean white/off-white background
- Subtle neutral borders
- High information density without feeling crowded
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Large editorial headlines
- Soft rounded cards
- Restrained shadows
- Tiny amount of gradient
- Minimal decorative elements
- No excessive glassmorphism
- No giant colorful blobs
- No cheesy factory illustrations
- No generic stock photos
- No cartoon business people
- No exaggerated 3D graphics

Use visual details such as:

- thin grid lines
- subtle technical diagrams
- tiny status indicators
- production metrics
- charts
- warehouse maps
- inventory rows
- machine status
- timeline elements
- table snippets
- scanning UI
- operational cards

The result should feel **precise, engineered and premium**.

# Final quality bar

Before finishing, evaluate the page against these questions:

1. Can I understand the product in 5 seconds?
2. Is it obviously for factories/workshops/warehouses?
3. Does it look significantly more modern than traditional ERP software?
4. Do the product visuals look like a real application rather than fake marketing graphics?
5. Is the value proposition about operational control, not a generic list of features?
6. Does the page feel premium enough that a serious industrial company could trust it?
7. Is the visual system consistent across every section?
8. Does mobile remain excellent?
9. Are animations subtle and purposeful?
10. Does the page create a clear reason to book a demo or start using the product?

Build the page to a **very high production-design standard**, with strong visual hierarchy, excellent spacing, consistent components, realistic product mockups, and polished responsive behavior.
